About Us

Chromelite Technologies Limited is a Kampala-based company specializing in branding, signage and structural steel supply. We provide creative, reliable and professional solutions to businesses and organizations across Uganda.

Our company is committed to delivering quality products, excellent customer service and innovative solutions. We combine creativity, technical expertise and modern technology to help businesses improve their visibility and achieve their objectives.

Our Services

Chromelite Technologies Limited offers a wide range of services designed to meet the branding, printing and construction needs of our clients.

Our experienced team focuses on quality, creativity and timely delivery in every project. Our major services include:

Signage

We design and produce professional business signs, shop signs, illuminated signs and promotional displays.

Vehicle Branding

We provide vehicle wrapping and branding services that help businesses advertise their products and services on the road.

Printing

We offer printing services for banners, posters, business cards, brochures and other promotional materials.

Steel Fabrication

We supply and fabricate structural steel products, metal frameworks and customized steel structures for different projects.

Our Projects

Chromelite Technologies Limited undertakes projects in commercial branding, signage installation, vehicle branding and structural steel fabrication.

Our projects are guided by attention to detail, quality materials and professional workmanship. We work closely with clients to understand their requirements and deliver solutions that reflect their business identity and objectives.
